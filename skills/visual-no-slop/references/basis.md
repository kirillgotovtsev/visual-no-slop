# Basis and limitations

Version 1.2.0; research date: 23 September 2026. Kirill Gotovtsev developed this method with Codex from a review of research and professional guidance on visual AI slop. The principal sources and the limits of their relevance are summarized below.

The fourteen principles form our working rubric, synthesized from that review. It is not a validated scale or a way to determine an image's provenance. The package includes the instructions and references needed to apply the method; no files from the author's computer are required.

## What supports the approach

- The study by Wadinambiarachchi and colleagues on [design fixation](https://samangi.com/papers/chi24-1021.pdf) provides grounds for checking a solution's dependence on the first visual cues. Results from a particular experimental task do not prove that all AI assistance worsens design.
- [T2I-CompBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06350) and [GenEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11513) show ways to check object relationships, counts and attributes separately. These are narrow measures of how images match prompts, not tests of overall artistic quality.
- [Bellaiche and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-023-00499-6) studied how attributed authorship affects assessment. A review should therefore distinguish a visible defect from attitudes toward the method of production.
- [W3C on decorative images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/decorative/) and [Bateman and colleagues on chart embellishment](https://doi.org/10.1145/1753326.1753716) help avoid a blanket ban on decoration. Research on particular charts does not prove that every embellishment is beneficial.
- [NN/g on grouping](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/common-region/) and [Norman on visual cues for action](https://jnd.org/signifiers-not-affordances/) support checking what relationships and promises form communicates.

## What these sources do not establish

No rule defines a list of bad typefaces, colors, grids or effects. Assess historical conventions and popular techniques against the task. Changes in fashion and model capabilities call for updating examples, not turning new tastes into prohibitions.

Campaign results, studio claims and critical essays have different evidentiary limits. They do not provide a numerical threshold for "AI slop." Checking the accuracy of subject and cultural details sometimes requires a source or specialist knowledge that the reviewer lacks.

Base changes to the skill on an observed failure in its application: unjustified activation, a missed mismatch, loss of the author's direction or excessive expansion of the task. Make a targeted correction to the rule and check it against the original situation and a closely related counterexample. Do not add a universal requirement because of a single failure.

## Refinement 1.1: aesthetic oversights in a technically sound layout

In its first application, the method missed impersonal typography and the accumulation of thin outlines. The rules checked legibility and individual relationships but did not require assessment of expressive character or combinations of techniques. This is an observed failure in application, not proof that all the original sources are unfit for purpose.

The refinements draw on a rereading of primary practical guidance:

- [Google Design: How to Choose a Web Font](https://design.google/library/choosing-web-fonts-beginners-guide) connects the choice with role, character, language, size and volume of text. This supports distinguishing expressive typography from calm functional typesetting; it does not justify banning an ordinary sans serif.
- [NN/g: Common Region](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/common-region/) directly discusses excessive borders and replacing them with space. [NN/g: Proximity](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/gestalt-proximity/) explains grouping through proximity. These inform the check for whether outlines are necessary while preserving relationships.
- [Anthropic: Improving frontend design through Skills](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills) and [frontend-design SKILL.md](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/41bbe19d1a1a7eaab5e7bb9050a417e5c6cffc8f/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md) discuss typographic character, recurring aesthetic defaults and the substantive role of visual structure. These are recommendations from the authors with examples, not an independent experiment on the effectiveness of all the advice. Their lists of typefaces and stylistic clichés have not been adopted as prohibitions.

The new mandatory passes, assessment of combinations and distinction between aesthetic findings and taste-based observations form our working procedure, synthesized from these sources. The sources do not establish how often a particular typeface or line appears in generated output and do not offer a way to prove authorship. Checking against a project and a counterexample exposes specific failures in the instructions; it does not validate a universal quality scale.
