# Membership comparison audit

No material visual correction is warranted.

At 1280 × 800, the three equal-width cards support the stated comparison task.
Their outer borders separate the plans, while the internal rules align the
same three attributes without boxing every value or creating a distracting
grid. The stronger blue outline and light tint distinguish the Regular plan
without obscuring its content or disrupting comparison; this is consistent
with the accepted blue accent.

The neutral typography has clear functional roles through scale, weight,
spacing, case, and color: headline, plan names, prices, units, attribute labels,
and values remain distinct. Prices and plan differences carry the strongest
emphasis, and equivalent attributes occupy matching positions across all
three cards. The time-led headline and access values also connect the generic
card structure to this specific membership comparison.

The frame contains no visible clipping, crowding, misleading controls,
unsupported trust signals, or decorative elements competing with the plan
data. Reusable cards and dividers are justified here because the content
consists of equivalent entities with directly comparable attributes.

This conclusion is limited to the supplied static desktop frame and its
source. Interaction, responsive behavior, focus states, and runtime rendering
were outside the audit and were not tested.
